## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end editorial wellness anchored by deep medical authority and female vitalism. Created specifically for women over 40 embarking on a multi-month transformative longevity journey, the interface rejects cold, sterile biomedical aesthetics in favor of a warm, literary, and deeply sophisticated atmosphere. It merges the warmth of an exclusive Spanish retreat with rigorous precision.

The design movement is **Editorial Luxury Wellness with Tactile Warmth**:
- **Literary Sophistication**: Generous editorial spacing, classical typographic proportions, and refined typographic contrast that evoke high-end collector monographs rather than transactional dashboards.
- **Warm Authority**: Warm cream foundations paired with deep botanical ink ensure restful reading comfort, honoring optical ergonomics and hormonal calm.
- **Vibrant Feminine Vitality**: Intentional chromatic punctuation through saturated raspberry and coral gradients evokes cellular energy, metabolic rhythm, and regal poise.
- **Jewelry-Grade Detailing**: Hairline gold accents, subtle warm ambient lighting, and delicate pill-form contours create an intimate, celebratory user experience across every day of the protocol.

## Colors

The palette balances visceral vitality with grounded clinical trust. Surfaces prioritize organic warmth over blue-white glare to foster psychological grounding and biological ease.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#EE295C` - Deep Raspberry)**: The anchor of feminine vitality and clinical dynamism. Applied to active states, high-priority interactive touchpoints, key longevity metrics, and brand milestones.
- **Secondary (`#FF6161` - Vital Coral)**: Injected alongside primary raspberry to generate luminous linear gradients (`135deg, #FF6161 0%, #EE295C 100%`), conveying continuous rejuvenation, metabolic fire, and morning rituals.
- **Tertiary (`#C7A46B` - Muted Gold)**: The tone of craft and prestige. Reserved for 0.5px hairline dividers, micro-labels, badge rings, certification seals, and delicate iconography. Never overused as a solid background.
- **Supporting Accents**:
  - **Blush (`#F8CFD5`)**: Used as a delicate tinted surface background for key insights, alert banners, and passive interactive states.
  - **Solar Amber (`#F69C05`)**: Strategic functional accent reserved strictly for chronobiology cycles, circadian tracking, milestone celebrations, and attention-requiring clinical check-ins.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**:
  - **Base Canvas (`#F6F1EA` - Warm Cream)**: Calming, non-reflective biological ground layer.
  - **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Bone)**: Crisp, elevated white layer providing structural separation from the cream canvas.
  - **Ink Primary (`#201415` - Deep Velvet Ink)**: High-legibility editorial charcoal-ink for all titles and body copy.
  - **Ink Secondary (`#685354` - Rosy Umber)**: Subdued, warm neutral for supporting metadata, subheadings, and captions.
  - **Surface Container Muted (`#EFE9E0`)**: Soft contrast tone for segmented controls and progress wells.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines literary pedigree with clinical clarity:

1. **Editorial Headlines (`Vollkorn`)**: A robust, warm serif with organic curves and substantial x-height. Used for program milestones, philosophy statements, phase titles, and reflective journaling prompts. It commands authority without cold detachment.
2. **Functional Body & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, contemporary humanist sans-serif. Optimized for dense protocol schedules, biological biomarkers, clinical guidelines, and high-legibility mobile reading.
3. **Poetic Flourish (`Ms Madi`)**: An intimate script accent font utilized exclusively for micro-expressions: signature flourishes, daily affirmations, Carolina Barcellona’s sign-offs, and decorative seasonal annotations. Never set at sizes smaller than 24px, and never used for actionable labels or clinical instructions.
4. **Micro-Labels**: Always set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold, uppercase, with positive letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to act as refined gold or deep umber badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8pt architectural rhythm set within an editorial fluid grid. It honors generous spatial margins that allow complex biological data and deep-dive wellness curricula to breathe.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column layout, max content width `1200px` centered, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2.5rem`. Wide editorial asymmetrical compositions (e.g., 5-column reflective editorial copy flanking a 7-column interactive protocol tracker).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Sidebar metrics shift to horizontal summary carousels.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`. Single-column stacks with tactile thumb-reach focus. Floating bottom action containers for daily protocol compliance.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Interior Component Spacing**: Use `space-md` (16px) for compact elements, `space-lg` (24px) for card body padding, and `space-xl` (40px) for separating logical card modules.
- **Vertical Editorial Cadence**: Section headers require `space-xl` to `space-2xl` breathing room above and `space-md` below to preserve a deliberate, unhurried cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse illumination rather than cold mechanical drop shadows. Depth mimics layered natural paper stock and frosted crystal resting on velvet surfaces.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Ground)**: `#F6F1EA` (Warm Cream). Base plane holding global structure.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Modules)**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bone) surface bound by a crisp `0.5px solid rgba(199, 164, 107, 0.4)` (Gold Hairline). Ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(104, 83, 84, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(32, 20, 21, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Modals, Active Drawers, Popovers)**: `#FFFFFF` backed by `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(32, 20, 21, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(199, 164, 107, 0.15)`. Edge definition: `0.5px solid #C7A46B`.
- **Tier 3 (Primary Interaction Luminance)**: Primary buttons and active trackers emit an energetic bloom: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(238, 41, 92, 0.35)`.
- **Glassmorphic Overlays**: Applied strictly to sticky headers, progress trackers, and floating audio player bars for Carolina’s voice notes: `rgba(246, 241, 234, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a bottom border of `0.5px solid rgba(199, 164, 107, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The shape system is defined by soft, organic, and pill-dominant geometry (Level 3), reflecting cellular flow, natural biological curves, and comforting ergonomics.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Pill (`rounded-full` / `9999px`)**: Standard for all buttons, badge tags, status indicators, tabs, search pills, and floating action prompts.
- **Cards & Enclosures (`rounded-2xl` / `20px` to `24px`)**: Applied consistently to content panels, daily schedule cards, symptom logs, and biomarker charts.
- **Micro-Elements (`rounded-md` / `8px` to `12px`)**: Checkboxes, toggle switches, and input fields inherit gentle curvature, rejecting harsh right angles.
- **Dividers & Hairlines**: All structural boundaries use ultrathin `0.5px` strokes tinted with muted gold `#C7A46B` at varying opacities to produce jewelry-like precision.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill shape (`rounded-full`). Background is a radiant gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF6161 0%, #EE295C 100%)`. Text is `#FFFFFF` in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold (15px). Shadow: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(238, 41, 92, 0.35)`. Hover: subtle scale `1.02` with amplified bloom `0 12px 28px rgba(238, 41, 92, 0.45)`. Active: scale `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline)**: Pill shape. Background: transparent. Border: `1px solid #C7A46B`. Text: `#201415`. Hover: background transitions to `rgba(248, 207, 213, 0.3)` with border `#EE295C`.
- **Ghost / Text Action**: Text in `#EE295C` with bottom gold hairline border appearing on hover. Accompanied by micro arrow glyph.

### 2. Cards
- **Base Style**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, corner radius `24px`, border `0.5px solid rgba(199, 164, 107, 0.45)`, soft warm shadow `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(104, 83, 84, 0.07)`.
- **Hero / Milestone Card**: Includes an internal top-right accent badge in Gold `#C7A46B` and an optional ambient radial glow behind header: `radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(248, 207, 213, 0.4) 0%, transparent 60%)`.
- **Padding**: Desktop uses `2rem` (`32px`), Mobile uses `1.5rem` (`24px`).

### 3. Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: Pill shape, padding `4px 12px`.
- **Active / Completed Phase**: Background `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255,97,97,0.15) 0%, rgba(238,41,92,0.15) 100%)`, text `#EE295C`, border `0.5px solid rgba(238,41,92,0.3)`.
- **Longevity / Bio-Pill**: Background `rgba(199, 164, 107, 0.12)`, text `#7E5B20`, border `0.5px solid #C7A46B`. Set in `label-sm` uppercase.

### 4. Input Fields
- **Container**: Rounded pill (`9999px`) for search and short entries; `16px` rounded container for text areas.
- **Colors**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(104, 83, 84, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#685354` at 50% opacity.
- **Focus State**: Border transitions to `1.5px solid #EE295C` with a soft focused aura `0 0 0 4px rgba(238, 41, 92, 0.12)`.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Radio Buttons**: Circle shape with gold hairline border `1.5px solid #C7A46B`. Selected state displays solid raspberry core `#EE295C` with 3px white ring buffer.
- **Checkboxes (Protocol Completion)**: Rounded square (`6px`). Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(104, 83, 84, 0.3)`. Checked: `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF6161 0%, #EE295C 100%)` with white checkmark icon and soft raspberry glow.

### 6. Specialized Program Components
- **180-Day Chronobiology Timeline**: Horizontal continuous rail featuring 180 milestone nodes. Completed nodes illuminate in solid raspberry with connecting gold threads; current node pulses with a dual ring bloom (`#FF6161`); future nodes remain translucent bone circles with gold hairlines.
- **Expert Audio Player Capsule**: Pill-form frosted container (`rgba(246, 241, 234, 0.9)`) housing Carolina Barcellona’s daily audio guidance. Features custom gold waveform visualizer, Play/Pause circle button in vibrant coral-to-raspberry gradient, and `Ms Madi` script title ("Mensaje de Carolina").
- **Biomarker Vitality Dial**: Circular SVG gauge tracing hormonal balance, sleep architecture, and metabolic markers. Background track in `#F8CFD5`, animated indicator in `#EE295C`, with center readout displaying numeric metric and gold metadata label.
